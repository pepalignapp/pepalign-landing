CONSUMER HEALTH DATA PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 07, 2026

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy supplements the Pepalign Privacy Policy and applies specifically to personal health and wellness information regulated under the Washington My Health My Data Act (MHMDA), Nevada SB 370, and applicable state consumer privacy statutes.

1. Categories of Consumer Health Data We Collect

Pepalign collects only the information you voluntarily input into the application to maintain your personal wellness records, including:

  • Health, wellness, and peptide protocol entries (compound names, doses, schedules, and your own notes).
  • Body metrics (weight, measurements, and check-in logs).
  • User-uploaded progress photographs.
  • Text inputs and queries submitted to interactive AI features (Pep-i and Describe Your Protocol).

2. Purpose of Collection and How We Use Health Data

We collect and process your consumer health data solely as necessary to provide the services you request, including:

  • Displaying your schedules, calculating reconstitution math, and triggering local on-device reminders.
  • Rendering visual logs, body metric charts, and user-generated PDF progress reports.
  • Processing prompt inputs in real time through our AI subprocessor (Google Cloud / Gemini API) to populate forms and answer user queries.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your consumer health data with data brokers, advertisers, or third-party marketing networks.

3. Third Parties and Subprocessors

To operate Pepalign, we disclose health data strictly to contracted service providers who process data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements:

  • Google Cloud Platform / Firebase: Secure database storage, authentication, and encrypted cloud asset storage.
  • Google Cloud AI (Gemini API): Real-time processing of user-submitted AI prompts. Inputs are processed to provide the feature and are not used to train AI models.
  • RevenueCat & Apple: In-app purchase verification and subscription status management (does not receive health logs).

4. Geofencing Restriction

Pepalign does not collect precise GPS geolocation and does not implement or use geofences around facilities providing health services to identify, track, or collect data from consumers.

5. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

Under Washington MHMDA, Nevada SB 370, and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we collect, process, or disclose your consumer health data.
  • Access the specific health data we maintain about you.
  • Withdraw consent for the continued collection or processing of your health data.
  • Request the permanent deletion of your consumer health data and account.

You can delete your account and all stored health logs inside the app at Profile → Settings → Delete account. You may also submit requests or appeal decisions by contacting us at support@pepalign.app.
